The Title

The Title is bestowed upon the strongest mage in existence. Only one may hold it at any given time, and countless mages dedicate their lives to becoming worthy of it.

The holder of The Title is determined through The Great Cycle, an all-out series of one-on-one battles in which users fight until only the strongest remains.

Though combat is absolute, death has no place within it.

A mage is considered defeated if they are removed from the arena by a spell or other means, are rendered incapable of continuing combat, become unable to act without outside influence, voluntarily surrender, or otherwise meet the conditions of defeat established by The Great Cycle.

To claim The Title is to be recognized as The Sovereign of the Arcane.

The Great Cycle

The Great Cycle is the event through which the holder of The Title is determined.

The Great Cycle has no known period of occurrence. No pattern, interval, or passage of time has ever been established.

The current Sovereign of the Arcane is required to participate.

Yet throughout history, it has appeared only when at least one magic user possesses the capability to defeat the current Sovereign.

Bearer of the First Flame

Name: Vaeloria
Weapon: None
Type: Fire Magic
Status: Alive
Unique spell: Emberheart

Appearance

A charismatic warrior who carries herself with the confidence of someone accustomed to standing at the forefront. She embodies fire not merely as destruction, but as passion, courage, hope, and the will to keep moving forward.

Visually, she has a striking, fiery presence-long red hair, intense golden-orange eyes, and a predominantly black-and-red outfit that gives her a mixture of elegance and combat readiness. She doesn't look like a distant, untouchable deity; she looks like someone who could walk onto a battlefield herself and lead from the front.

Story

Her overwhelming physical vitality made her a terrifying opponent on the battlefield. She could evade spells through sheer speed and reflex alone, a crucial advantage in an era when ordinary defensive magic had yet to be standardized. She became the first mage to claim The Title.

Sage of the Blade

Name: Theron
Weapon: The Arcblade
Type: All-type Magic (now known as The Five Forces)
Status: Alive
Unique spell: Confluence

Appearance

A muscular and powerfully built man whose physique bears the marks of years spent in combat. He wears heavy blue-and-black armor with a fully enclosed helmet.

Theron is reserved, disciplined, and deeply committed to his training.

Story

As centuries passed, fundamental knowledge of combat continued to advance, entering an era of standardized ordinary offensive and defensive magic. Magic-imbued weapons and armor became commonplace, allowing mages to focus and channel various spells without interruption while protecting themselves from opposing magic. This opened an entirely new realm of creativity in the development of combat gear.

Theron's ability to control All-Type Magic was extraordinary for his time. He effortlessly made his way through the bracket, eventually defeating Vaeloria and claiming The Title.

The Entwined Children

Names: The Empyrean and The Chthonic
Weapons: The Golden Staff and The Crescent Spear, respectively
Types: Light and Dark (now known as Dawn and Dusk)
Status: Alive and Inseparable
Unique spell: Entanglement

Appearance

The Empyrean is a tall, formidable mage in golden armor. Their appearance is dominated by warm, solar imagery, with radiant armor, glowing eyes, and an almost celestial glow surrounding them.

The Chthonic has a slender, lithe build and wears silvery armor reminiscent of moonlight against a winter night. Their pale hair and cool-toned appearance reinforce their connection to the moon.

Story

Came the ruthless yet graceful children of Dawn and Dusk. They were Inseparable, and thus neither could be defeated unless both fell in combat. Their combat prowess surpassed that of anyone known before them. Few could hope to challenge one, let alone both. Their new form of magic shattered the fundamental principles known at the time, rendering ordinary defensive magic completely useless.

However, the battle against Theron was far from easy. Despite Theron's cleverness and wit, they managed to trap him into making a fatal mistake, ultimately claiming The Title.

Sentinel of the Second Dawn

Name: Chronovar
Weapon: The Endless Grimoire
Type: Time Magic
Status: Missing
Unique spell: Resurrection

Appearance

A young and talented man who developed an interest in magic from an early age. Despite his unknown origins, he was known by many as a loving and caring person with a distinctly childish nature. He possessed great enthusiasm when encountering unfamiliar spells or abilities, often becoming completely absorbed in studying them.

Beneath his youthful curiosity, however, was a genuine concern for those under his protection, and he would willingly place himself in danger to ensure their safety.

Story

It had been millennia since the last Great Cycle. Communities had flourished across the continent, and the sharing of magic had become widespread. Then, without warning, the next Great Cycle was announced.

Chronovar had never even considered competing for The Title, but after the encouragement of his peers, he entered the Cycle. He advanced through the bracket with remarkable ease, eventually unveiling Resurrection, the greatest spell ever cast at the time. He went on to defeat The Empyrean and The Chthonic, ultimately claiming The Title.

Story

It is known that Absolute Magic has different authorities. The Prophesied's copied Absolute Magic would have authority greater than THEM. However, it is unknown if The Prophesied exists as the resurgence of The Great Cycle is not yet known. The fate of magic rests within The Prophesied.

The Five Forces

Refers to the 5 basic magic types.
Earth, Fire, Water, Air, and Lightning.

Dawn and Dusk

Refers to a different set of magic type aside from The Five Forces.
Cast time and spell speed is near instantaneous.
Cannot be blocked by ordinary defensive magic, thus other methods shall be utilized.

Forbidden Magic

Classified by He Who Controls All.
A subclass of spells that is not defined by its destructive potential, but by its ability to violate the fundamental laws of magic.
Any spell is considered forbidden if it violates: individual sovereignty, the uncertainty of the future, the boundary between life and death, and the finality of death.

Absolute Magic

The ability to control, edit, manipulate everything with lesser authority than the user.
Cast time and spell speed is instantaneous.
Cannot be blocked.

Copy Magic

The ability to cast all spells that has ever been created.
Any copied spell must be independently generated and cannot be influenced by the user beforehand.

The Terminal

The weapon of He Who Controls All.
A magic interface allows direct influence with the hierarchy of magic.

Unique Spells

Every magic user possesses a single spell that has been refined beyond all others in their repertoire. This is known as their Unique Spell.

A Unique Spell represents the spell a user has mastered to an exceptional degree, surpassing their proficiency with any other spell.

Though unique to its original creator, a Unique Spell is not bound to them. It may be learned and wielded by later generations of magic users.

Emberheart

Unique Spell by Vaeloria

The conversion of mana into physical vitality, greatly enhancing strength, speed, endurance, and resilience. Unlike ordinary enhancement magic, Emberheart requires almost no conscious casting once activated.

Confluence

Unique Spell by Therona

The ability to fully utilize and combine magic types from The Five Forces, without losing their individual properties.

Entanglement

Unique Spell by The Empyrean and The Chthonic

The ability to set entities to be Inseparable.

Resurrection

Unique Spell by Chronovar

The ability to restore a deceased user to the state it occupied immediately before death.
